The **Modelcraft Y-3009 Mini-Servo** is ideal for use in R/C models or for interfacing with microcontroller systems such as PICAXE, Arduino, Raspberry Pi etc.

- (L x W x H) 23 x 12 x 27 mm

- A low cost, high quality servo

- Supplied with control horns

+-----------------------+--------------+
| Bearings              | Plain        |
+-----------------------+--------------+
| Control               | Analogue     |
+-----------------------+--------------+
| Maximum Output Torque | 20 Ncm       |
+-----------------------+--------------+
| Operating Speed       | 0.08 sec/60° |
+-----------------------+--------------+
| Supply Voltage        | 4.8 to 7.4 V |
+-----------------------+--------------+
| Weight                | 11 g         |
